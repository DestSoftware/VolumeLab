Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

In short: VolumeLab does not collect, store or share any personal data. The app works entirely on your device and has no user accounts. The only third party that may process data is Google AdMob, which serves the ads shown in the app.

1. Who we are

VolumeLab ("the app") is developed by Dest Software ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles information. It applies to the VolumeLab Android application.

2. Data we do not collect

We do not collect, transmit or store any of the following:

  • Name, email address, phone number or any account information (the app has no sign-up);
  • Location data;
  • Contacts, photos, files, messages or any other personal content;
  • Audio recordings or the sound played on your device;
  • Analytics, usage statistics or crash reports sent to us.

3. Data stored only on your device

Your preferences - such as selected volume levels, custom presets, schedules, chosen language and theme - are saved locally on your device using the Android DataStore mechanism. This information never leaves your device, is not visible to us, and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

4. Permissions and why they are used

  • Audio settings - to read and change the volume of the system audio streams you control.
  • Bluetooth - to detect the name/type of a connected Bluetooth audio output so the correct device profile can be shown. This permission is declared as "never for location"; it is not used to determine your location.
  • Display over other apps (optional) - only requested if you choose to enable the floating volume panel. It is never requested otherwise and can be revoked at any time in system settings.
  • Notifications / foreground service (optional) - only used while the floating panel is running, so it can stay on screen and be closed reliably.
  • Internet / network state - required to load advertisements.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve, measure and prevent abuse of ads, Google and its partners may collect and process data such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, general (IP-based) location, device and app information, and your interactions with ads. This processing is carried out by Google as an independent controller - we do not receive your personal data; we only see aggregated, anonymous revenue reports.

For more information, see Google's How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, a Google-certified consent message is shown before any ad request. You can change or withdraw your choices at any time from Settings > Ad privacy options inside the app.

6. Your controls

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Turn off personalised advertising in the same Android settings.
  • Change your ad consent choices from within the app's Settings screen.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored preferences.

7. Data sharing and retention

We do not sell or share any data, because we do not collect any. Because we store nothing, there is nothing for us to retain. Data processed by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's own policies.

8. Children

VolumeLab is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

9. Changes to this policy
